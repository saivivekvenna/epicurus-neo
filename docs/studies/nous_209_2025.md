## Locked semantics

Nous-209 encodes 209 shared frameshift peptides and immunogenicity was tested with 16
multi-antigen pools. Figure 2c gives each of 37 evaluable participants' number of reactive pools
(range 1–16); all 37 were source-called positive at peak. It does not identify which pools reacted
for any participant. These become 37 patient-level Event-B observations with no candidate ID.

Supplementary Table 3 lists 115 deconvolved immunogenic FSP identifiers and their global pool
membership. The adapter preserves 115 FSP identifiers, 16 pool entities and 115 `CONTAINED_WITHIN`
relationships. It does not connect those identifiers to patients because the mapping is absent.
No component receives a positive or tested-negative label.

The deconvolution table contains no FSP row for Pool15 and spells Pool7 once as `Pool 7`. The
adapter normalizes the harmless space, preserves all 16 assay-pool entities, and reports that
Pool15 has no membership edge rather than inventing a component.

Four of 37 participants had baseline spontaneous pool responses, but the public outcome table does
not identify them. No patient-level Event-A call is manufactured. HLA, MSI lesion identity and tumor
FSP confirmation remain unavailable at patient-candidate resolution.
