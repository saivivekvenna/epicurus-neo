# Dynamic upstream gate — falsification ledger

Chronological record of what was tried and what the data said. Nothing here is tuned to the LOCKED
CheckMate test; entries below are DEV-only unless stated.

| # | variant / hypothesis | result | kept? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pure-EL percentile gate** (incumbent from the pool-size diagnostic) | Retains only 66.7% (Gartner) / 35.7% (IMPROVE) of positives at the 25%-negative pool. Baseline to beat. | baseline |
| 2 | **AND-of-independent-vetoes** (el ∧ prime ∧ expr, missing→KEEP) | **Pareto-dominates** the pure-EL gate on positive retention at every matched negative-removal level on Gartner/IMPROVE; ties/edges on multimer (FEASIBILITY.md). Adopted as Layer 1. | **YES** |
| 3 | **expr as a required veto axis** (3-way AND) | Identical to #2 on full-coverage dev cohorts, but disables the gate entirely on CheckMate (81% expr-missing → every candidate auto-kept). Rejected in favor of **core={el,prime} + expr rescue-only**, which is byte-identical on dev and still operates on the external cohort. | replaced by #4 |
| 4 | **core veto {el,prime} + expression rescue-only** | Adopted. Preserves the frozen confidence-only expression policy (expr never vetoes alone, never reweights the ranker) and keeps the fail-open guarantee on the core axes. | **YES** |
| 5 | **Single global threshold `t` calibrated on pooled dev positives (target 0.95)** | Dragged to `t=0.25` by IMPROVE's spread positives → removes only 2–10% of negatives. Safe but modest. This is the frozen deployment config. | frozen |
| 6 | **Aggressive per-cohort operating points to hit 50–75% removal** | To remove ~50% of negatives, retention collapses (IMPROVE 0.45–0.62, Gartner 0.85–0.91). Violates the ≥95% safety bar. The 50–75%@≥95% target is **unreachable** on peptide-only features. | **FALSIFIED** |
| 7 | **Premise: a SAME-FEATURE presentation gate improves downstream top-20** | **FALSIFIED (scoped).** Threshold sweep: at any safe retention (≈1.0) the paired downstream Δhits@20 ≈ 0. **Circularity audit** (CIRCULARITY_AUDIT.md) shows why this is a tautology, not a general result — see #9. | **FALSIFIED (this feature class only)** |
| 8 | **CP retention lower bound ≥ 0.95 as a universal bar** | Sample-size-capped: `CP-LB(46/46) = 0.937`, so Gartner (46 positives) can never certify ≥0.95 regardless of the gate. The bar is only meaningful on the larger cohorts (IMPROVE 467). Reported as an underpowering limit, not an unsafety. | noted |
| 9 | **Circularity audit — v1 veto axes vs downstream ranker inputs** | v1 veto axes {EL, PRIME} ARE the dominant inputs to genuine PRIME and (PRIME-dominated) frozen Epicurus. Empirically: **0** gate-removed candidates ever sat in a ranker top-20 (gartner/improve; multimer 1, a saturated small-pool artifact), **0%** of the high-EL/high-PRIME decoy stratum removed, Spearman(keep-margin, ranker) ≈ +1. ⇒ zero top-20 effect is a STRUCTURAL consequence of feature overlap. **Corrects #7**: the falsification is scoped to same-feature presentation gating; a gate on orthogonal features is a different, untested hypothesis. | scoping correction |
| S1 | **Sequence-only orthogonal residual gate** (independent experiment, not ours: `scripts/hard_decoy_gate_experiment.py`) | **FALSIFIED.** Veto from peptide n-grams + allele/anchor tokens + length/chemistry (PRIME/EL only for the stratum, not veto). Catastrophic cross-study collapse: train-Gartner→test-IMPROVE **retained 1.5%** of positives (removed 198/201); train-IMPROVE→test-Gartner retained 12.8% (removed 34/39). Peptide/HLA recognition motifs encode severe study/assay domain shift; within-study CV is misleading. ⇒ v2 FORBIDS sequence-motif vetoes; requires leave-one-study-out + OOD abstention. | **FALSIFIED**; forced OOD abstention into v2 |
| P1 | **Orthogonal probe (multimer, IN-SAMPLE)** — do agretopicity/foreignness/processing/dissimilarity/TPM separate decoys from positives in the hard-decoy stratum? | Faint in-sample signal: cross-fitted leave-one-PATIENT-out AUROC **0.59**, ~9% of stratum negatives removable at ≥95% retention (24 pos / 1181 neg). In-sample (multimer = frozen-Epicurus training), underpowered, NOT leave-one-study-out ⇒ mechanism whisper, not validation. | inconclusive; motivates v2 on real WES/RNA |

## v2 — NET-utility reselection (reframed objective: maximize top-20 hits, positives may be sacrificed)

| # | variant | result | kept? |
|---|---|---|---|
| V0 | **Backfill/diversity feasibility probe** | Backfill supply is thin (positives at ranks 21-40 avg 0.46/0.90/0.11 per patient; positives are DEEP, not just outside top-20). Label-blind **diversity** levers HURT (allele-cap −0.61 IMPROVE; exact-dedup ≈0) — redundant-decoy crowding is not the bottleneck. Only expression is separable on Gartner (pos 9 vs neg 6), flat on IMPROVE (5.26 vs 5.41). | probe → no easy win |
| V2a | **Fixed-budget negative-risk reselection** (discordance + expr + interaction; NOT PRIME/EL directly; budget by inner CV; outer LOSO) | Gartner Δ **+0.038**, IMPROVE **−0.016**, multimer **−0.053**. **Decisive control: random-matched-pool pruning MATCHES or BEATS it** (Gartner +0.038–0.069, IMPROVE −0.016) ⇒ the learned selection adds nothing over shrinking the pool. | **FALSIFIED** (no transfer; beaten by random) |
| V2b | **Counterfactual top-20 replacement w/ uncertainty** (remove a top-20 candidate only if replacement-q LCB > removed-q UCB; meant to auto-abstain OOD) | Did NOT abstain (removed 77/139/43): Gartner **+0.077** (= denominator+25mer confound), IMPROVE **−0.115**, multimer **−0.210**. Cause: bootstrap-logistic ensemble is OVER-CONFIDENT (LCB≈UCB) ⇒ conservative gate collapses to the mean. Lesson: genuine OOD abstention needs conformal/distributional uncertainty, not bootstrap variance; with correct abstention the best achievable here is DO NOTHING (=ungated). | **FALSIFIED as-implemented**; design needs calibrated uncertainty |
| V2b′ | **Harm-calibrated counterfactual** (gain-margin = smallest whose inner-CV harm rate ≤5%, applied to held-out) | Recovers safety PARTLY: Gartner & multimer → 0 removals, Δ **0.000** (correctly abstains — discards the illusory Gartner +0.077, avoids multimer −0.210). But IMPROVE STILL harms (**−0.082**, 52 removed) because the harm structure is itself study-shifted (a margin calibrated on training studies under-controls harm on a held-out study). ⇒ true safety needs per-held-out OOD abstention (feature/regime router), not a globally-calibrated margin. | safety recovered 2/3; confirms OOD wall |
| V2c | **Independent HistGBT direct-utility** (not ours: `scripts/dynamic_utility_gate_experiment.py`) | Gartner **+0.077** (2 improved/24 tied/0 harmed), IMPROVE **−0.029**, multimer **−0.115**. Same regime-specific Gartner-only pattern; 25mer confound flagged by its author. | corroborates: no transfer |

**v2 synthesis:** five independent angles (V0 probe, V2a, V2b, V2c, plus the sequence-only #S1) agree — **no source-invariant negative-risk signal exists among the top ranks** in the current cohorts; the recognition wall holds at top-20. Gartner's only positive numbers are a **denominator × 25-mer-regime** artifact that random-matched pruning reproduces. **No v2 frozen.** Deliverable = best Pareto behind a feature-availability / peptide-length-regime OOD router with CALIBRATED abstention (never route by study label). Data limitation: a valid v2 needs **minimal-peptide-regime (8-11mer) cohorts with orthogonal WES/RNA features** (Miller IPV `PRJNA980652`; Gartner reconstruction restricted to class-I minimal epitopes). Artifacts: `V2_REPORT.md`, `dynamic_gate_v2.json`; code `src/event_b/dynamic_gate_v2.py`, `scripts/dynamic_gate_v2.py`, `scripts/dynamic_gate_v2_feasibility.py`.
